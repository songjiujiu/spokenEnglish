=== PDF PAGE 2 ===
        Two months ago you got a job as an editor for the 
magazine Designs & Fashions. But now you find that the work 
is not what you expected. You decide to quit. Write a letter to 
your boss, Mr. Wang, telling him your decision, stating your 
reason (s), and making an apology. 
        Write your letter with no less than 100 words.  
        Do not sign your own name at the end of the letter; use “Li 
Ming” instead. 
        You do not need to write the address.  
 
 
 

=== PDF PAGE 3 ===
Dear Mr. Wang, 
        Thank you very much for having offered me such a golden 
opportunity to work as an editor for Designs & Fashions, a 
best-selling magazine renowned for its extraordinary quality 
and taste. I was thrilled by the news and have done my utmost, 
but after two months‟ work I feel obliged to resign, which is 
truly a tough decision for me. 
 

=== PDF PAGE 4 ===
亲爱的王先生： 
       非常感谢您能为我提供这么宝贵的机会在因卓越的质量和品位而
著称的畅销杂志社《设计与时尚》担任编辑一职。当初获悉此事时我
欣喜万分，并在工作中尽了自己最大的努力，但是在两个月后我无奈
不得不辞去此工作，这对我来说实在是个艰难的决定。 
 

=== PDF PAGE 5 ===
        Lack of relevant experience is the main reason behind my 
resignation. Enthusiastic as I was about this position, I seemed 
never to perform beautifully. The longer I worked, the more 
frustration I felt, both at my incompetence in this job and at 
falling short of your expectations. Too much commuting time is 
another reason. I had to tolerate at least a two-hour journey 
each day to get to work and rise at 5 o‟clock in the morning, 
which was so exhausting that I suffered from chronic fatigue.  

=== PDF PAGE 6 ===
       缺乏相关的工作经验是我辞职的主要原因。尽管我对我的工作充
满了热情，却似乎一直无法完美地完成工作。我工作的时间越久，挫
折感就越强烈，不仅是对于我自己的无能，而且也是对于辜负了您殷
切的希望。另一个原因是上下班路上的时间过长。我每天上班至少要
花费2个小时在路上并且每天五点钟就要起床，这是如此使人筋疲力
竭以至于我一直以来感到很疲劳。

appreciated and I apologize profusely for the inconveniences 
caused both to you and to Designs & Fashions before and after 
I quit this job. Please let me know if there is something I can do 
to compensate for it. 
Yours sincerely, 
Li Ming 
 

=== PDF PAGE 8 ===

带来的不便深表歉意。如果我可以做些什么来弥补请和我联系。 
您诚挚的 
李明

换为great; excellent; marvelous; once-in-a-lifetime）（to do

noted; notable; prestigious; celebrated） 
 extraordinary: adj. 非凡的（可换为remarkable; exceptional; 
outstanding）

=== PDF PAGE 18 ===
［知识点］让步状语从句中的强调句 。其构成是：将被强调词置于从
句句首，从句原来的连词去掉，在被强调词后加上as，其余成分顺序
排列在后面。这种从句一般位于主句之前 ，而且多用于书面语中。例
如： 1 、 Although she is beautiful, I still don‟t like her.  
Beautiful as she is, I still don‟t like her. 尽管她很美丽，我还是不
喜欢她。2、Although I much admire Newton as a scientist, I 
don‟t like him as a man.  Much as I admire Newton as a 
scientist, I don‟t like him as a man. 尽管牛顿作为一个科学家我非
常尊敬他，但是作为人我不喜欢他。
